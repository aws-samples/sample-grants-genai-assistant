Team, Environment, and Institutional Resources: Biobehavioral Mechanism Study of Neuropsychiatric Symptoms in Alzheimer's Disease

1. PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR AND TEAM EXPERTISE

The Principal Investigator designed and executed the pilot study described in the Preliminary Data, establishing feasibility of a combined caregiver-report, salivary biomarker, and wrist actigraphy protocol in an outpatient memory clinic population, with 79% enrollment yield from eligible approached patients and 94% actigraphy wear-time compliance across the pilot's 7-day protocol. This work directly informs the expanded biomarker panel and extended 14-day monitoring protocol proposed in this application. 2. CLINICAL RECRUITMENT ENVIRONMENT

The outpatient memory clinic that served as the pilot recruitment site provides an existing population of patients with probable AD dementia across the mild-to-moderate severity range needed for this study, along with established clinical infrastructure for cognitive staging (Clinical Dementia Rating determination) that will be used for eligibility screening in the full-scale study. The pilot's demonstrated feasibility of enrolling patients during routine clinical visits, without requiring a separate research-only visit, supports the recruitment approach proposed for the expanded 80-participant sample.

3. BIOMARKER ASSAY INFRASTRUCTURE

The salivary alpha-amylase assay platform validated in the pilot (enzymatic kinetic assay, intra-assay coefficient of variation 8.2%) is established and will be used unchanged in the full-scale study to preserve comparability with pilot data.
